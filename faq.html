FAQS

Questions,
answered simply.

Do I need to create an account?+

No. The initial booking flow is designed to start without website account registration.

What does Health Insurance Query Assistance include?+

It covers general explanation of health-insurance terms and processes within the scope of the selected service.

Can you fill my proposal form for me?+

The service can explain questions and information requested. Customers remain responsible for providing accurate factual answers.

Can you guarantee that an insurance claim will be approved?+

No. The service does not guarantee claim approval or reimbursement.

Can I share medical or policy documents?+

Only when necessary for the selected service and after the appropriate consent process. Information should be kept secure and limited to what is necessary.

Do you sell insurance policies?+

The initial model focuses on education and assistance. Any regulated insurance distribution activity requires the appropriate regulatory structure and authorization.

How will I know the status of my request?+

A paid request can be assigned a reference/ticket number and a stated response timeframe.
